# Refund policy

Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase for any order that has not been used. Digital subscriptions are refunded pro rata for the unused part of the billing period.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds usually appear on the customer's statement within 5 to 10 business days. Wallet credit is available immediately.

## Approval limits

Support agents can approve refunds up to 200 euros without escalation. Refunds above 200 euros need approval from a team lead, and refunds above 2,000 euros need approval from finance.

## Chargebacks

When a customer opens a chargeback with their bank, the support team does not issue a separate refund. Finance responds to the dispute with the order evidence within seven days.

## Partial refunds

Partial refunds are allowed for damaged or missing items. The agent records the reason code so that the warehouse team can track packaging problems.
